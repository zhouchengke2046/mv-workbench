# 场景空镜 45° 俯视样板(zh/en 双语)

### IMG-S01 场景甲·空镜

**用途**:场1/场7 复用主景;固定地理关系。

#### zh
```text
【用途】场景空镜板,供视频生成作为同一地点的地理参考。
【画风】写实建筑摄影,明亮干净,材质与结构清晰;不插画、不概念图。
【版式】16:9 横幅,大全景,45° 俯视构图,空间完整入画。
【地理锚点】<空间类型与功能+边界+入口/路线+固定锚点(柜台/柱/窗/机器)及相对关系,左右以前/中/后景参照>。
【光线】<自然光/室内光/仪表光>为主,写明光向;无戏剧性彩色光。
【背景】画面边界为<远山/天空/墙面>,不出现画外环境暗示。
【文字政策】画面无文字、无水印、无 logo。
【负面】不要人物、动物、车辆;不要卡通/Q版;不要平面俯视图(必须 45° 透视);不要 HDR 过饱和。
```

#### en
```text
[Purpose] Empty scene plate to serve as the geographic reference for the same location in video generation.
[Style] Photorealistic architectural photography, bright and clean, clear materials and structure; no illustration, no concept art.
[Layout] 16:9 wide shot, large establishing view, 45-degree high-angle composition, the whole space in frame.
[Anchors] <space type and function + boundaries + entrances/routes + fixed anchors (counter/column/window/machine) and their relative positions, using foreground/midground/background references instead of ambiguous left/right>.
[Lighting] <natural/indoor/instrument> light as the key source with a stated direction; no dramatic colored light.
[Background] The frame boundary is <distant mountains/sky/wall>; no hints of off-frame environment.
[Text policy] No text, watermark or logo.
[Negative] No people, animals or vehicles; no cartoon/chibi; no flat top-down map view (must be 45-degree perspective); no oversaturated HDR.
```
