# 角色三视图样板(zh/en 双语)

### IMG-C01 角色甲·三视图身份板

**用途**:锁定同一人的脸、体态与服装;三格分别供正脸/正面全身/背面引用。

#### zh
```text
【用途】人物身份板三视图,供视频生成锁定同一人的脸、体态与服装。
【画风】写实商业人像摄影,浅灰无缝背景,均匀柔光,真实皮肤与布料材质;不插画、不概念图。
【版式】16:9 横向三格并列:①正面面部特写(头肩,含发型与眉眼);②正面全身,头部完全不入画,画面顶部从锁骨以下开始,不出现脸、头发、脖颈断面;③背面全身(示发型后界与服装背面)。三格同一人、同一服装、同一光线,比例一致、脚底对齐;不要四视图。
【身份锚点】二十六七岁男青年,短黑发,眉目干净无胡须,体型偏瘦;浅灰蓝工装夹克(左胸小圆徽章)+白T+深色长裤;"浅灰蓝夹克+小圆徽章"为固定辨识组合,任何镜头不得增删改。
【姿态】自然站姿,双臂垂放,双脚与肩同宽,中性平静表情。
【背景】纯浅灰色无缝背景,无环境、无家具、无地面投影。
【布光】正面大面积柔光平铺,无戏剧性侧光。
【文字政策】画面无文字、无水印、无 logo;徽章只作小圆色块。
【负面】不要卡通/Q版/畸形/低质/磨皮;不要换装;不要胡须或长发;不要第四视图;中格不得出现头、脸、头发、脖颈断面;不要道具。
```

#### en
```text
[Purpose] Character identity sheet in three views, to lock one face, physique and outfit for video generation.
[Style] Photorealistic commercial portrait photography, plain light-grey seamless background, even soft light, real skin and fabric; no illustration, no concept art.
[Layout] 16:9 horizontal three-panel: (1) frontal face close-up (head and shoulders, showing hairline and eyes); (2) frontal full body with the head fully out of frame - the panel starts below the collarbone, no face, hair or neck stump; (3) rear full body showing the back of the hair and the jacket. Same person, same outfit, same lighting, consistent scale, feet aligned; no four-view.
[Anchors] Male in his mid-to-late twenties, short black hair, clean-shaven, slim; light blue-grey work jacket (small round badge on the left chest), white tee, dark trousers; the "light blue-grey jacket + small round badge" combination is fixed and must not be added to or removed.
[Pose] Natural standing, arms at sides, feet shoulder-width, neutral expression.
[Background] Plain light-grey seamless background; no environment, furniture or floor shadow.
[Lighting] Flat frontal soft light; no dramatic side light.
[Text policy] No text, watermark or logo; the badge is a plain round color patch only.
[Negative] No cartoon/chibi/deformed/low quality/plastic skin; no costume change; no beard or long hair; no fourth view; the middle panel must not show head, face, hair or neck stump; no props.
```
