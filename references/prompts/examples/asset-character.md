# 角色三视图样板（IMG-C · zh/en 双语）

### IMG-C01 小晚·三视图身份板

**用途**：锁定同一人的脸、体态与服装；三格分别供正脸/正面全身/背面引用。

#### zh
```text
【用途】人物身份板三视图，供视频生成锁定同一人的脸、体态与服装。
【画风】写实商业人像摄影，浅暖灰无缝背景，均匀柔光，真实皮肤与布料材质；不插画、不概念图。
【版式】竖版三格纵向排列：①正面头肩特写（完整五官与发型）；②正面全身，头部完全不入画，画面顶部从下巴/领口以下开始，不出现脸、头发、脖颈断面；③背面全身（示发型后界与服装背面）。三格同一人、同一服装、同一光线，比例一致、脚底对齐；不要四视图。
【锚点】26 岁中国女生，圆润小脸，杏眼，淡妆；及肩黑发微卷中分；米色宽松针织开衫+白色内搭+浅灰直筒裤+帆布单肩包；"米色针织开衫+帆布包"为固定辨识组合，任何镜头不得增删改。
【姿态】自然站姿，双臂垂放，双脚与肩同宽，中性平静表情。
【背景】纯浅暖灰无缝背景，无环境、无家具、无地面投影。
【布光】正面大面积柔光平铺，无戏剧性侧光。
【文字政策】画面无文字、无水印、无 logo。
【负面】不要卡通/Q版/畸形/低质/磨皮；不要换装；不要第四视图；中格不得出现头、脸、头发、脖颈断面；不要道具。
```

#### en
```text
[Purpose] Character identity sheet in three views, to lock one face, physique and outfit for video generation.
[Style] Photorealistic commercial portrait photography, plain light-warm-grey seamless background, even soft light, real skin and fabric; no illustration, no concept art.
[Layout] Portrait three stacked panels: (1) frontal head-and-shoulders close-up with full features; (2) frontal full body with the head fully out of frame - the panel starts below the chin/collar, no face, hair or neck stump; (3) rear full body showing the back of the hair and the outfit. Same person, same outfit, same lighting, consistent scale, feet aligned; no four-view.
[Anchors] Chinese woman, 26, round face, almond eyes, light makeup; shoulder-length black hair with soft curls, middle part; beige loose knit cardigan + white top + light grey straight trousers + canvas shoulder bag; the "beige knit cardigan + canvas bag" combination is fixed and must not be added to or removed.
[Pose] Natural standing, arms at sides, feet shoulder-width, neutral expression.
[Background] Plain light-warm-grey seamless background; no environment, furniture or floor shadow.
[Lighting] Flat frontal soft light; no dramatic side light.
[Text policy] No text, watermark or logo.
[Negative] No cartoon/chibi/deformed/low quality/plastic skin; no costume change; no fourth view; the middle panel must not show head, face, hair or neck stump; no props.
```
