# 道具多视图样板(zh/en 双语)

### IMG-P01 道具甲·多视图

**用途**:全片核心道具;锁定轮廓、材质与功能结构。

#### zh
```text
【用途】道具多视图板,供视频生成锁定同一物体的外形与结构。
【画风】写实产品摄影,产品级细节,哑光与金属质感真实;不插画、不概念图。
【版式】1:1 正方形多视图并排:左侧正视图+右侧侧视图(必要时加顶视图或局部特写);同一物体、同一光照、同一比例;视图之间无图注、无引线、无尺寸标注。
【结构锚点】<轮廓+主比例+主材质与配色+功能结构(接缝/扣件/开口/握持面)+有识别价值的长期磨损>;任何镜头不得增删改。
【光线】柔和三点布光,无强反光斑。
【背景】纯浅灰色无缝背景,无台面、无投影。
【文字政策】画面无文字、无水印、无 logo。
【负面】不要手、人物或环境;不要卡通/Q版;不要多件复制品;不要改变主色或材质。
```

#### en
```text
[Purpose] Multi-view prop plate to lock the shape and structure of one object for video generation.
[Style] Photorealistic product photography, product-grade detail, realistic matte and metal materials; no illustration, no concept art.
[Layout] 1:1 square with views side by side: front view on the left, side view on the right (add top view or detail close-up if needed); same object, same lighting, same scale; no captions, leader lines or dimension marks between views.
[Anchors] <silhouette + main proportions + primary materials and colors + functional structures (seams/fasteners/openings/grip areas) + long-term wear that aids recognition>; must not be added to or removed.
[Lighting] Soft three-point studio light; no strong highlights.
[Background] Plain light-grey seamless background; no surface, no shadow.
[Text policy] No text, watermark or logo.
[Negative] No hands, people or environment; no cartoon/chibi; no duplicate copies; no change of primary color or material.
```
